## 2. What didn't

- Save eligibility was initially enforced unevenly across Configure, reusable
  saves, paired Apply and bootstrap. A correct editor did not guarantee a correct
  publication caller. Whole-diff reviews exposed this missing shared invariant
  after earlier slice acceptance; the shared saver and bootstrap repair addressed
  the cause ([ledger](../../archive/2026-10-08-playbook-config-ui/execution.md)).
- Provider-page inference and HTML parsing produced repeated rejected candidates,
  including false confirmation and false refusal. S7a moved task-fit claims to
  edition-reviewed statements with fresh original-source confirmation; subsequent
  parser repairs retained withdrawal and byte-preservation checks. No comparative
  superiority or total-cost conclusion follows from those repairs.
- Host/privacy controls were incomplete. An enforced direct sandbox could not
  perform authenticated discovery; actual hosts ran with manually bounded full
  access. A custom review captured forbidden history and interrupted a fixture
  run, so its report remained nonqualifying. The privacy-capture failure and
  selected-field repair remain recorded. See [host limits and dated
  reconciliation](../../archive/2026-10-08-playbook-config-ui/host-qualification.md).
- Repeated S2/S4/S7 and whole-diff repairs required escalation and explicit route
  decisions. Cumulative failures were retained, not reset across handoffs. Two
  custom qualification attempts are exhausted; shipment grants no third attempt.
- Successful Apply retains recovery artifacts that bootstrap did not ignore.
  Publishing them could expose configuration evidence; deleting them would lose
  completion/conflict proof. This is the separately authorised product follow-up,
  not a reason to change recovery semantics in the retro.

| Anti-pattern | Observed shape and disposition |
|---|---|
| Nodding — no independent verification | Avoided for final acceptance; fresh probes rejected real defects. A contaminated review cannot count merely because its technical checks passed. |
| Amnesiac — no persistence | Archived candidates, failures and private receipts prevented resets. Thin recoverable `/learn` evidence is a gap; this retro and field report persist the available lessons. |
| Manual — no scheduling | Retro was explicitly deferred and stayed in pending closeouts. Monthly drift became due; its separate maintenance owner completed October in PR #25 (outside this feature period). No missing scheduler or new telemetry is inferred. |
| Blind — no discovery | Early source/provider assumptions and same-value preset proof were insufficient. Genuine discovery, reviewed-source confirmation and a differing-Plan walkthrough supplied the missing evidence; allowances and performance remain unknown. |
| Tangled — no isolation/handoff | Full-access host boundaries and forbidden history capture weakened isolation. Fresh artifact-only review, portable source identities and exact-candidate receipts mitigated this; they did not establish filesystem enforcement. |

   does not authorise future substitutions.
3. Keep retained recovery artifacts private while preserving completion/conflict
   evidence → separate bootstrap/upgrade product follow-up; no new retro code.
